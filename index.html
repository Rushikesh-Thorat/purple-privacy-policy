Privacy Policy

Purple: Focus Study Timer
Last updated: September 10, 2026

Purple: Focus Study Timer ("Purple", "we", "our", or "the app") is a study and productivity application designed to help users focus, manage time, and track their study sessions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Handle

Focus and app data

Purple stores information needed to provide its features, such as focus sessions, timers, countdowns, progress information, milestones, settings, themes, and wallpapers. This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to use Google Sign-In for backup-related features, Purple may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your Google account identifier and email address. We use this information only to authenticate your account and provide the requested backup and restore functionality.

Google Drive backups

If you choose to use Google Drive backup or restore, Purple accesses the Google Drive storage necessary to create, read, and restore backups requested by you. Backup files may contain your app data, including focus history, settings, and other information stored by the app.

Backups created by Purple are encrypted before being uploaded. Purple does not use your Google Drive files for advertising or sell your data.

Purchases

Purple may offer optional digital theme or wallpaper purchases through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Purple does not receive or store your full payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by Purple is used to:

  • Provide and operate the app's focus, timer, progress, countdown, theme, and wallpaper features.
  • Authenticate you when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Create, restore, and manage backups when you request those features.
  • Remember your app settings and purchased or owned digital content.
  • Provide purchased digital content through Google Play Billing.

3. Data Sharing

Purple does not sell your personal information.

When you use optional Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup features, information is processed through Google's services as necessary to provide those features. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Purple does not use your information for targeted advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

App data is primarily stored locally on your device. Backup files created by Purple are encrypted before upload. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup features. You can also use the app's core focus and productivity features without using cloud backup.

You may remove locally stored app data by using the device's app controls to clear or uninstall the app. Cloud backups stored in your Google Drive can be managed through your Google account and Google Drive.

6. Children's Privacy

Purple is a general productivity and study application and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

7. Third-Party Services

Purple uses third-party services where necessary to provide optional functionality, including Google Sign-In, Google Drive, and Google Play Billing. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
